How it works

01
Watch & score

A two-model cascade: a classifier flags which invoices will slip, then a regressor estimates by how many days — only for the flagged ones. Informational only.

02
Act on a deterministic ladder

Days-beyond-terms picks the rung — courtesy → firm reminder → early-settlement offer → payment plan → stop. A pure function. The LLM never chooses the action.

03
Diagnose & draft

A local model (Ollama — no API, no egress) labels why the invoice is slipping and writes the buyer email. Five hard bounds refuse over-reach and escalate.

04
Hand you the brief

Plus an append-only audit trail — pick any action, trace exactly why: the score, the diagnosis, the rung, the bounds it passed, the message.
